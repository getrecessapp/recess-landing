🫡 The basics

Recess is a game that sends you into the real world to complete quests. By using it you agree to play fair, follow the law, and use common sense. You must be 13 or older to have an account.

🚨 Be safe out there

Quests happen in real places. Stay aware of your surroundings, obey traffic and trespassing laws, and never put yourself or anyone else in danger to finish a quest. You're responsible for your own safety — Recess is not.

🤫 Your account

Keep your login details to yourself. You're responsible for activity on your account. Don't impersonate others, cheat verification, harass people, or post anything illegal, hateful, or harmful.

🤑 XP & rewards

XP is for status and progress in the game — it has no cash value and can't be transferred or sold. Venue rewards are not live yet; when they launch, redemption terms will apply and may change.

🤳 Content you post

You own your photos. By posting, you give Recess a license to display them in the app to people you've shared them with. Don't post content you don't have the right to share.

✌️ Changes & ending

We may update these terms or change the app over time. You can stop using Recess and delete your account anytime in Settings. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.
