📦 What we collect

Your account info (name, email, handle, birthday), the photos you submit to complete quests, and — only if you turn it on — your location. We also collect basic usage data to keep the app working.

📍 Your location

Location is off until you enable it. We use it to show nearby quests and, if you opt into sharing, to show your spot to friends. You can turn location and sharing off anytime in Settings.

⚡ How we use it

To run the game: place quests near you, verify quest photos, track XP and streaks, and connect you with friends. We don't sell your personal data, full stop.

🤖 AI verification

When you complete a quest with a photo, or post a sidequest, we send that photo or text to our third-party AI provider (OpenAI) for a quick yes/no check (that the photo fits the quest, and to screen posts for safety). They process it only for that check — they don't use it to train their models or for advertising, and we never sell it. You're asked to consent the first time, and you can decline (you just won't be able to post or complete photo quests).

🤳 Your photos

Quest photos are stored with your account and shown only to the people you choose to share them with.

🙈 Who sees what

You control visibility. Private accounts and per-quest visibility settings decide who sees your activity. Friends only see what you allow.

🕹️ Your control

You can edit your info, change sharing settings, or delete your account and its data anytime in Settings. Questions? Reach us at getrecessapp@gmail.com.
